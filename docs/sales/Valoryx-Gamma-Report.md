# Valoryx — The Offline-First POS & Business Suite for Indian Retail
Run your entire shop — billing, stock, suppliers, staff, and reports — from one app that keeps working even when the internet doesn't.
A modern point-of-sale + back-office platform built for kirana stores, supermarkets, and multi-outlet chains.

## The Problem Shops Face Today
- Most billing apps **die when the internet drops** — and the counter stops with them.
- Shop owners juggle **paper, Excel, and 3–4 different tools** for billing, stock, suppliers, and payroll.
- Month-end numbers are **untrustworthy** because returns, pending payments, and expenses live outside the system.
- Cheap mobile apps are slow on a real counter; full ERPs (Tally, Marg) are heavy, expensive, and hard to learn.
- The result: lost sales, stock errors, no audit trail, and decisions made on guesswork.

## What Valoryx Is
- A **single, offline-first platform** that runs the whole shop end-to-end.
- Take a sale, hold a pending bill, accept a return, receive a supplier delivery, log an expense, transfer stock between branches, run payroll, and read a clean audit trail — **without ever opening Excel**.
- Works as a **desktop app (Windows/Linux), web app, tablet, and mobile** — same data everywhere.
- **Offline-first core:** bills on local storage in ~0.7 ms, then syncs to the cloud automatically in the background.
- Multi-tenant and secure by design: every shop's data is fully isolated.

## What We Have Today — Core Features
- **Billing / POS:** GST & non-GST invoices, card/list product view, thermal (80mm) printing, UPI QR with amount + bill number pre-filled.
- **Pending Bills & Khata:** invoice now, collect later; partial payments and per-customer credit.
- **Returns & Exchanges:** structured returns that correctly reverse stock and link to the original bill.
- **Inventory & Stock Transfer:** branch-aware stock, in-transit tracking, barcode label printing.
- **Suppliers & Purchases:** supplier master, delivery notes, purchase orders (auto-numbered), one-click stock receipt.
- **Customers:** customer master linked to bills, with loyalty and contact details.
- **Expenses & Payroll:** category-tagged expenses, employees, attendance, advances, and payroll with trend charts.
- **Reports & Dashboard:** real-time sales, profit margins, top products, and peak-hours analytics.

## Built for Trust — Security & Reliability
- **Offline-first:** the counter keeps billing during internet outages; data syncs when the connection returns.
- **Blazing fast:** routes respond in **1–20 ms** (vs 3–9 seconds on always-online competitors).
- **Full audit trail:** every create/update/delete is logged per shop; nothing is silently destroyed.
- **Role-based access:** owner / manager / admin / staff / cashier, with fine-grained server-side permissions.
- **Hardened:** 2FA (TOTP), forced password change, IP & session tracking, and a security regression test suite.
- **Auto-updating desktop app:** new versions install themselves — no manual reinstalls.

## How It Works — 5 Simple Steps
- **01 — Install:** one desktop install (or open in a browser). Works instantly, online or offline.
- **02 — Set up:** add products, customers, and suppliers — or import existing data.
- **03 — Sell:** fast counter billing with barcode, UPI QR, and thermal printing.
- **04 — Manage:** stock, suppliers, expenses, staff, and payroll all in one place.
- **05 — Grow:** real-time reports and dashboards turn daily data into better decisions.

## The Market — Who This Is For
- **15M+** small/kirana shops in India (Segment A).
- **2M+** mid supermarkets & chain shops (Segment B — our primary target ⭐).
- **Regional chains** with 5+ outlets needing centralized control (Segment C).
- India's retail digitization is accelerating: GST compliance, UPI adoption, and the shift away from paper are all tailwinds.
- The sweet spot: shops that have **outgrown a mobile app but can't afford or learn a heavy ERP**.

## How We Compare — The Crowd
- **Vyapar / myBillBook (₹2K–₹5K/yr):** mobile-first, cloud-dependent, weak on desktop and offline reliability.
- **Marg ERP / Tally (₹8K–₹25K+):** powerful but heavy, expensive, and hard for a shopkeeper to use.
- **GoFrugal / Ginesys (₹10K–₹30K+):** supermarket-grade but enterprise-priced and complex to deploy.
- **Valoryx sits in the gap:** more capable than the cheap mobile apps, far simpler and cheaper than the ERPs.

## Why Valoryx Wins — Our Edge
- **Offline-first is our moat:** competitors break when the internet drops — Valoryx doesn't.
- **Desktop reliability + cloud sync** in one product, not an either/or.
- **One app for the whole shop** instead of 3–4 disconnected tools.
- **10–100× faster** counter experience than always-online apps.
- **Fair price for real depth:** ERP-grade workflows without ERP price or complexity.
- **Margin advantage:** offline installs cost us almost nothing to run, so we can price aggressively and still profit.

## Useful for the Business — The Value
- **Stop losing sales:** the counter never goes down, even offline.
- **Trustworthy numbers:** returns, pending payments, and expenses all flow into true month-end profit.
- **Less manual work:** no more paper, no more Excel reconciliation.
- **Better decisions:** live dashboards show top products, margins, and peak hours.
- **Lower risk:** full audit trail + role permissions protect against theft and mistakes.
- **Scales with the shop:** start with one counter, grow to multiple outlets on the same system.

## Pricing — Land and Expand

- **🟢 Starter — ₹2,999/yr:** one counter, unlimited bills, GST + offline + printing.

- **Phase 2 — Soft launch:** Free tier live, launch-discount paid tiers, direct outreach to 100 shops in 2 cities.
